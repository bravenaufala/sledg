# Icy Downhill Slider — MVP Design

## Engine & Tech
- Godot 4.x (GDScript only)
- 3D third-person downhill endless slider

## Phase 1: Greybox Scope (Current Goal)
- 1 scene (`main.tscn`) with a long, sloped icy track (low friction).
- Player: Simple capsule/box sled that slides down automatically with gravity.
- Controls: A/D or Left/Right arrows to steer smoothly with slight banking/tilt.
- Camera: Third-person follow camera positioned behind and slightly above the player.
- Visuals: Plain grey/blue CSG shapes or primitives only. No art, audio, or UI yet.

## Rules for AI
- Keep scripts minimal and focused on feel/physics.
- Do not add menus, score systems, or procedural chunk spawning until Phase 1 is approved.


## Phase 4 & Checkpoint B: Japanese Water-Ice to Golden Hayfield Overhaul
- **3 `BiomeData` Presets (`res://data/*.tres`)**:
  - `alpine_ice.tres` (**KYOTO WATER-ICE**, `biome_id = "kyoto_ice"`): Glossy icy-cyan WebGL-safe shader track, snow-dusted pines, glowing stone lanterns, occasional vermilion Torii gates (`y >= 9.0` clearance), `ice_friction = 0.10`, `slope_angle_deg = -16.0`, cool alpine sky, icy mist spray `Color(0.82, 0.96, 1.0)`.
  - `autumn_canyon.tres` (**GOLDEN HAYFIELD**, `biome_id = "golden_hayfield"`): Warm sunlit golden-straw/dirt matte `StandardMaterial3D` track, wooden ranch fences, stacked golden round hay bales, occasional pitched-roof red barns, `ice_friction = 0.17`, `slope_angle_deg = -18.0`, golden-hour sky, golden straw spray `Color(0.96, 0.80, 0.35)`.
  - `midnight_glacier.tres` (**MIDNIGHT SHRINE**, `biome_id = "midnight_shrine"`): Dark obsidian-blue glossy shader ice track, glowing cyan rails, dark pine silhouettes, warm/cyan paper lanterns, dark-vermilion Torii gates, `ice_friction = 0.06`, `slope_angle_deg = -16.0`, deep indigo night sky, glowing cyan frost spray `Color(0.45, 0.92, 1.0)`.
- **Dynamic Distance-Based Biome Transitions & Transition Torii Gates**:
  - `Main` cycles automatically through `[alpine_ice, autumn_canyon, midnight_glacier]` every `1500m` (`biome_cycle_distance = 1500.0`).
  - Transitions update `TrackSpawner` (`set_biome()`), `Player.ice_friction`, `Player.set_spray_color()`, `RoadsideProps` (spawning a guaranteed grand `TransitionToriiGate` across the chunk ahead), smoothly lerp `WorldEnvironment` sky colors, and show `"ENTERING: <BIOME NAME>"` on HUD.
- **Visual Polish & Web Export Readiness**:
  - Web-safe `res://shaders/water_ice.gdshader` (albedo, low roughness `0.08–0.10`, high specular sheen, Fresnel rim highlight, procedural UV+TIME shimmer) plus visual-only low-poly `RoadsideProps`, compatible with the `gl_compatibility` WebGL renderer.
