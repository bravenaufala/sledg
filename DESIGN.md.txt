# Icy Downhill Slider — MVP Design

## Engine & Tech
- Godot 4.x (GDScript only)
- 3D third-person downhill endless slider

## Phase 1: Greybox Scope (Current Goal)
- 1 scene (`main.tscn`) with a long, sloped icy track (low friction).
- Player: Simple capsule/box sled that slides down automatically with gravity.
- Controls: A/D or Left/Right arrows to steer smoothly with slight banking/tilt.
- Camera: Third-person follow camera positioned behind and slightly above the player.
- Visuals: Plain grey/blue CSG shapes or primitives only. No art, audio, or UI yet.

## Rules for AI
- Keep scripts minimal and focused on feel/physics.
- Do not add menus, score systems, or procedural chunk spawning until Phase 1 is approved.


## Phase 4: Biomes, Dynamic Transitions & Visual Polish
- **3 `BiomeData` Presets (`res://data/*.tres`)**:
  - `alpine_ice.tres`: Crisp icy blue track (`#a8d8f0`), slate rails (`#5c7080`), crimson obstacles (`#c0392b`), `ice_friction = 0.12`, `slope_angle_deg = -16.0`, cool alpine sky (`#3b6998` top / `#a8cce0` horizon).
  - `autumn_canyon.tres`: Warm amber/terracotta track (`#d37543`), deep canyon rails (`#6e2c1b`), dark rock obstacles (`#4a2311`), `ice_friction = 0.18`, `slope_angle_deg = -18.0`, warm sunset sky (`#8e3b2e` top / `#f39c42` horizon).
  - `midnight_glacier.tres`: Dark obsidian track (`#1c2430`), glowing cyan rails (`#00e5ff`), magenta neon hazards (`#ff2a6d`), `ice_friction = 0.06`, `slope_angle_deg = -16.0`, deep night sky (`#090e1a` top / `#182b49` horizon).
- **Dynamic Distance-Based Biome Transitions**:
  - `Main` cycles automatically through `[alpine_ice, autumn_canyon, midnight_glacier]` every `1500m` of distance (`biome_cycle_distance = 1500.0`).
  - Transitions update `TrackSpawner` (`set_biome()`), `Player.ice_friction`, active chunk materials and slope angles, smoothly lerp `WorldEnvironment` procedural sky colors, and flash an `"ENTERING: <BIOME NAME>"` HUD banner.
- **Visual Polish & Web Export Readiness**:
  - Stylized low-poly sled (`MeshInstance3D` deck, tapered `PrismMesh` nose, twin metallic runners, rider + helmet) and faceted 6-sided low-poly spire obstacles (`CylinderMesh` tiered spire).
  - Configured `gl_compatibility` renderer (`rendering/renderer/rendering_method` and `rendering_method.mobile`) for clean Web / itch.io export.
