# Icy Downhill Slider — MVP Design

## Engine & Tech
- Godot 4.x (GDScript only)
- 3D third-person downhill endless slider

## Phase 1: Greybox Scope (Current Goal)
- 1 scene (`main.tscn`) with a long, sloped icy track (low friction).
- Player: Simple capsule/box sled that slides down automatically with gravity.
- Controls: A/D or Left/Right arrows to steer smoothly with slight banking/tilt.
- Camera: Third-person follow camera positioned behind and slightly above the player.
- Visuals: Plain grey/blue CSG shapes or primitives only. No art, audio, or UI yet.

## Rules for AI
- Keep scripts minimal and focused on feel/physics.
- Do not add menus, score systems, or procedural chunk spawning until Phase 1 is approved.
